# See a dermatologist promptly
Changing, bleeding, asymmetric or fast-growing mole or spot; non-healing sore; painful cystic acne or scarring; sudden severe rash, swelling of lips/face, difficulty breathing (emergency); signs of infection (pus, fever, spreading redness); rash that does not improve after stopping a product; persistent hair loss or patches.

# Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Avoid retinoids (retinol, adapalene, tretinoin) and high-strength salicylic acid. Generally considered safer options: gentle cleanser, moisturizer, mineral sunscreen, azelaic acid. Always confirm with the doctor.

# Allergies and reactions
Stop the product if burning, swelling or hives appear. Patch test new products. Never mix many new actives together.

# Limits of this assistant
It gives general skincare guidance, cannot diagnose skin disease and does not replace a dermatologist.
