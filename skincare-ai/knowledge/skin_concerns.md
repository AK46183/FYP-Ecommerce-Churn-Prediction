# Oily skin
Shiny by midday, enlarged-looking pores. Use a gentle gel cleanser, a lightweight non-comedogenic moisturizer (do not skip it) and sunscreen. Niacinamide or salicylic acid can help. Over-washing makes oil worse.

# Dry skin
Tight, flaky, rough. Use a creamy non-foaming cleanser, hyaluronic acid on damp skin, ceramide moisturizer. Avoid hot water and strong foaming cleansers.

# Combination skin
Oily T-zone, normal or dry cheeks. Gentle cleanser, lightweight moisturizer; treat zones differently (acid only on T-zone).

# Sensitive skin
Stings or reddens easily. Keep routine to 3 steps, fragrance-free products, introduce one new product at a time, patch test behind the ear for 24-48 hours.

# Mild acne
Whiteheads, blackheads, few pimples. Gentle cleanser, salicylic acid or niacinamide, light moisturizer, sunscreen. Do not pick or squeeze; it causes marks and scars. Give any new routine 6-8 weeks.

# Moderate to severe acne
Many inflamed, deep or painful pimples, cysts, scarring. Should be seen by a dermatologist; over-the-counter products alone are often not enough.

# Dark spots and post-acne marks
Marks after pimples fade slowly. Daily sunscreen is essential; vitamin C, alpha arbutin, niacinamide, azelaic acid help over 2-3 months.

# Dullness
Often dehydration, no sunscreen or dead-cell build-up. Hydration, vitamin C in the morning, gentle exfoliation once or twice a week.

# Dark circles and puffiness
Can be genetics, sleep, allergies or thin skin. Eye creams give modest improvement; sunscreen, sleep and cold compress help.

# Basic routine order
Morning: cleanser, serum if any, moisturizer, sunscreen. Night: cleanser, treatment (acid/retinoid/serum), moisturizer. Introduce only one active at a time.
