# Wedding or event skin plan
(Also: shaadi, shadi, bridal, dulhan, function, barat, walima.)
Ask how much time is left. More than 8 weeks: build a simple steady routine (cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen, one treatment) and stay consistent. 4 to 8 weeks: start any new product now, one at a time, patch tested. 2 to 4 weeks: stop trying new products and strong actives; focus on hydration, sleep and sunscreen. Last 7 days: use only products your skin already knows; no new facial, no peels, no scrubbing, no waxing or threading on the face the day before. Drink water, sleep 7-8 hours, eat fruit and vegetables.

# Stress, tiredness and skin
(Also: tension, preshan, anxiety, neend, thakan, sar dard.)
Stress, poor sleep and low water intake can make skin look dull, tired and break out. Simple help: regular sleep, water through the day, gentle cleansing and daily sunscreen. Be kind and warm when someone shares stress; skin care is only one small part of feeling better.

# How to apply products
(Also: kaise lagana, kab lagana, kitna lagana, routine ka tareeqa, day plan, subah raat.)
Morning order: cleanser, serum, moisturizer, sunscreen last. Night order: cleanser, treatment or serum, moisturizer. Cleanser: wet face, massage 30 to 60 seconds with lukewarm water, rinse. Serum: 2 to 3 drops on slightly damp skin, wait about a minute. Moisturizer: a pea to coin-sized amount on face and neck. Sunscreen: about two finger-lengths for face and neck, every morning, reapply after 2-3 hours in sun. Patch test any new product behind the ear for 24 to 48 hours.

# Skincare by age
(Also: umar, age, saal ki, teenager, 20s, 30s, 40 ke baad, budhapa, jhuriyan.)
Teens (13-19): oil and acne are common; use a gentle gel cleanser, light moisturizer, sunscreen, and salicylic acid or niacinamide for spots. Avoid retinol and strong peels. Under 13: only a gentle cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen, with a parent's help. 20s: keep it simple; daily sunscreen is the most important step, add vitamin C or niacinamide for glow or marks. 30s: skin renews more slowly; add a retinol product at night (start 2-3 nights a week), peptides, and keep sunscreen daily. 40 and above: skin gets drier and thinner; use a richer moisturizer, gentle cleanser, retinol or peptides at night, and sunscreen every morning. Retinol is never for pregnancy or breastfeeding.
