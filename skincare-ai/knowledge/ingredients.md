# Salicylic Acid (BHA)
Oil-soluble acid that goes into pores. Helps blackheads, whiteheads and mild acne on oily or combination skin. Start 2-3 times a week; can dry or sting. Avoid combining with strong retinoids at the same time at the start. Patch test first.

# Niacinamide
Vitamin B3. Helps oil balance, visible pores, uneven tone and mild redness. Usually well tolerated by most skin types. 2-5% is enough for most people.

# Hyaluronic Acid
Humectant that pulls water into the skin. Good for dry and dehydrated skin. Apply on slightly damp skin and seal with a moisturizer.

# Ceramides
Skin-barrier lipids. Help dry, sensitive or irritated skin and skin damaged by acids or retinol. Good base for any routine.

# Vitamin C
Antioxidant used in the morning. Helps dullness and dark spots over weeks. Can irritate sensitive skin; always follow with sunscreen.

# Alpha Arbutin
Gentle pigment-reducing ingredient for dark spots and post-acne marks. Results take 8-12 weeks. Works best with daily sunscreen.

# Retinoids (retinol, adapalene)
Help acne, texture and ageing signs. Use at night, start 1-2 nights a week, expect dryness at first. Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Always use sunscreen next morning.

# Azelaic Acid
Helps acne, redness and dark spots. Fairly gentle; mild tingling is common.

# Benzoyl Peroxide
Kills acne bacteria. Can dry the skin and bleach fabrics. Start with low strength, spot or short contact use.

# AHA (glycolic, lactic, mandelic acid)
Surface exfoliants for dullness and rough texture. Use 1-2 times a week at night, not on broken or very sensitive skin.

# Sunscreen
Most important step for dark spots, acne marks and ageing. Use SPF 30+ every morning, about two finger-lengths for face and neck, reapply every 2-3 hours when outdoors. Needed even on cloudy days and indoors near windows.
